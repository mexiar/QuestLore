# Leveling guides tell you what to do. QuestLore tells you why.

Following a leveling guide keeps you moving, but it can leave the story behind. Who are you helping? Why does this assignment matter? What brought your character into this conflict?

**QuestLore is a companion for guided leveling**, giving you a little context while you follow your next objective. Stay on your route and stay connected to Azeroth.

## The story, right beside your quests

QuestLore adds a **Lore** section below your quest tracker, with a short narrative summary of your active quest. Each story explains the situation, the people involved and the purpose of your assignment in a format you can read while playing.

The narratives focus on what your character knows at the current quest step, avoiding revelations from later parts of the chain.

There are **5,259 quest narratives per supported language**, including Forever-specific quests.

## Click a name. Discover its story.

Who is that character? What is that place? Why does that organization matter?

Recognized names appear in **yellow**. Click one to open an independent encyclopedia article, then follow related names inside it to learn more.

The encyclopedia includes **2,163 entities and 15,141 articles across seven languages**, covering characters, places, organizations, objects, peoples, events and concepts, including Forever-specific content.

Links work in quest summaries, story history and race/class introductions. Articles open in the narrative language, in a movable, resizable reader, without changing your active quest.

## Make Lore fit your screen

Keep Lore attached below your quest tracker, or detach it and place it wherever you prefer.

- **Adjustable text size** for comfortable reading.
- **Optional black background with a transparency slider**, from fully transparent to opaque, for better readability over the game world.
- **Move and resize** the detached window, or make it wide and short to create a banner above your action bars or across the top of your screen.
- **Minimize and restore** the section when you need more space.
- Save your display preferences and detached window position and size **per character**.

The background is disabled by default, keeping the transparent tracker-style appearance until you choose otherwise.

## Your adventure has a history

Revisit previously read stories in **Options → AddOns → QuestLore → Story history**.

The archive displays a continuous feed, **newest first**, with each quest title and narrative separated by a line. Encyclopedia links work throughout the feed, so you can explore names from earlier adventures too.

Keep up to **200 unique stories per character**, including completed and abandoned quests. Reading history leaves your active quest summary unchanged.

## Read the full quest when you want more

Choose **Read full quest text** to open the original description and objectives in your game client's language, with scrolling and a movable, resizable window. Its position and size are saved per character.

Prefer a cleaner display? You can hide the **Read full quest text** button in the addon settings. It is shown by default, and the preference is saved per character.

Full text is available while the quest remains in your log, including when you open it from history. The archive keeps summaries rather than permanent copies of original quest text.

If a quest has no usable summary, QuestLore shows a clickable preview of its original text ending in **…**.

## Follow your guide without losing the story

QuestLore works alongside **Polestar, Questie and RestedXP**.

Optional direct integrations let Lore follow the active quest from Polestar or RestedXP. Questie works through the usual quest selection and focus controls.

**An active quest you select or focus takes priority over the guides.** When that focus is cleared or the quest leaves your log, QuestLore can resume following your guide.

Guide integrations can be enabled or disabled in the addon settings. QuestLore also works independently.

## Find your place in Azeroth

Before a new character's first assignment, QuestLore introduces their place in the world through a single story combining **race and class**.

It connects your character's background with their calling, giving you a reason to care before the first quest begins.

## Language support

- **Full support: English and Latin American Spanish.** Complete narrative coverage of the supported quest catalog, with source-based encyclopedia articles.
- **Community Polish translation:** all 5,259 quest summaries and 90 race/class introductions, contributed by **[dawiss](https://github.com/dawiss)**. The current interface is also available in Polish. Polish encyclopedia articles are provisional English-based translations and retain English proper names and article titles.
- **Partial support: French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Russian.** Summaries and encyclopedia articles are provisional translations from English and retain **English proper names**. Encyclopedia titles also remain English; these banks have sampled editorial review rather than complete native-language review.

The displayed **quest title** and **original quest text** use your game client's language, independently of the narrative language.

Automatic narrative selection uses Latin American Spanish for Spanish clients and English for other clients. All seven languages can be selected manually. The interface language has its own setting, separate from the narratives.

## See what changed after an update

After you install a new QuestLore version, a small notice below Lore lets you open its release notes. Dismiss it when you are done, or disable update notices in the settings.

You can always read the last five versions’ notes in **Options → AddOns → QuestLore → What’s new**, in your interface language, with a copyable link to the full changelog on CurseForge. Acknowledged versions do not repeat their notice.

## Compatibility and setup

Created by [@alejandrosena](https://x.com/alejandrosena) for **World of Warcraft: Forever, interface 16001**.

**ForeverCore** enables attachment below the quest tracker. Without it, QuestLore works as an independent window. **Polestar, Questie and RestedXP are optional.**

Open **Options → AddOns → QuestLore** to configure the addon, or type `/lore` to show its window.
