# Leveling guides tell you what to do. QuestLore tells you why.

Following a leveling guide makes it easy to keep moving, but sometimes you lose track of the story behind the route. Who are you helping? Why does this task matter? What brought your character into this conflict?

**QuestLore is a companion for guided leveling**, helping you stay connected to the world while following your next objective.

## A little context, right beside your quests

QuestLore adds a transparent **Lore** section below your quest tracker, with a short narrative summary of your active quest.

Each summary explains the situation, the people involved, and the purpose of your assignment. It is practical enough to read while questing, keeping the story close without interrupting your adventure.

The narratives focus on what your character knows at that quest step, avoiding revelations from later parts of the chain.

## Read the full quest when you want more

Want to go beyond the summary? Select **Read full quest text** to open the quest's original description and objectives from your current quest log, in the language of your game client.

The reader supports scrolling, adjustable text size, and a movable, resizable window. Its size and position are saved per character.

Full text is available while the quest is in your log. Your story history keeps the summary rather than a permanent copy of the original quest text.

## Follow your guide without losing the story

QuestLore works alongside **Polestar, Questie, and RestedXP**:

- **Polestar:** optional direct integration follows the accepted quest associated with its active navigation target.
- **Questie:** follows quests you select or focus through the normal quest journal and tracking controls.
- **RestedXP:** optional direct integration follows the accepted quest associated with its arrow. When the arrow has no identifiable quest, a current guide step with one unambiguous accepted quest can provide the context.

Enable or disable the guide integrations in the addon settings. Without a usable guide target, QuestLore follows your selected or tracked quest. If both direct guide integrations are enabled, Polestar takes priority.

## Language support

- **Full support: English and Latin American Spanish**, with complete narrative coverage of the supported quest catalog.
- **Partial support: German, Brazilian Portuguese, and French.** These provisional summaries are translated from the English narratives and retain **English proper names and quest titles**. They are not based on original quest text collected in those languages, and their linguistic review is incomplete.

Each language bank covers **5,259 quest narratives**, including Forever-specific quests. Full support describes narrative coverage; the audit of original localized Spanish proper names is still in progress.

Automatic selection uses Latin American Spanish for Spanish game clients and English for other clients. Choose any supported language manually in the options. The full-text reader always uses your game client's language, independently of your summary language.

If a quest has no usable summary, QuestLore shows a short preview of its original text in your game client's language, ending in **…**. Click it to read the full quest. Original-text previews are not saved in story history.

## Find your place in the world

At the beginning of a new character's adventure, QuestLore introduces your place in Azeroth through a single story combining your **race and class**.

It connects your character's cultural background with their calling, giving you context before the first assignment.

## Your adventure has a history

Revisit previously read stories in the addon options, including quests you have completed or abandoned.

The archive keeps up to **200 unique stories per character**. Reading an older entry leaves your active quest summary unchanged.

## Features

- **5,259 quest narratives per supported language**, including Forever-specific quests.
- Automatic language selection and a manual language selector.
- Original full quest descriptions and objectives for quests in your current log.
- Adjustable text size.
- A transparent, minimizable section attached below the quest tracker.
- An optional detached window that you can move and resize, with a wide banner layout.
- Per-character settings, window geometry, and story history.
- Local narrative data, with no online AI or translation service during play.
- **Free and open source** under the MIT License.

## Compatibility

Designed for **World of Warcraft: Forever, interface 16001**. Compatibility with Retail or other Classic clients has not been verified.

**ForeverCore** enables attachment below the quest tracker. Without it, QuestLore works as an independent window. **Polestar, Questie, and RestedXP are optional.**

Open **Options → AddOns → QuestLore** to configure the addon, or type `/lore` to show its window.
